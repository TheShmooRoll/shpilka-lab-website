OUR RESEARCH

Cells survive by coordinating the organelles inside them.

We investigate how organelles detect problems, communicate stress and remodel their function. A major focus is the relationship between mitochondria and peroxisomes, and how these pathways shape aging and disease.

01

Peroxisomal stress

How defects in peroxisomal protein import, quality control and metabolism are sensed and communicated to the rest of the cell.

02

Mitochondria ↔ peroxisomes

How stress in one organelle changes the biology of the other, and how inter-organelle communication supports adaptation.

03

Proteostasis & aging

How organelle quality-control systems preserve function over time — and what happens when those systems fail.
